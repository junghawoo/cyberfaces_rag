# Next Steps in Unix/Linux

## Text content

### Continued Learning of Unix/Linux
Your next steps to continue learning more of Unix/Linux would be to dive deeper into the command line and get a stronger grasp on what is really going on, and how it all works. Another thing you could do would be to try out different Linux distributions. Each has its own advantages and operates ever so slightly different from the next. Knowing how to use Unix/Linux systems and knowing how they operate is a major part in understanding how various computers and systems function.

### More Linux Distributions
Here are a variety of different Linux distributions available for download. Their websites contain a lot of information about them and how they operate.
