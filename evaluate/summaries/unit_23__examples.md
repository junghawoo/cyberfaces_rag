# Examples

Module focused on applications implemented in GEMPACK. Contains fetched resources from external research networks and platforms: Journal of Global Economic Analysis (jgea.org), MyGeohub/GLASSNET (mygeohub.org/groups/glassnet) — a global research network addressing Sustainable Development Goals with focus on agricultural workforce, biophysical impacts, and climate change extremes; and Global Trade Analysis Project (GTAP, www.gtap.agecon.purdue.edu) at Purdue University's Center for Global Trade Analysis and Department of Agricultural Economics.

Key content includes: GTAP Data Base (with GTAP Africa 1 and Africa 2 databases covering 39-42 regions and 57 sectors); Journal of Global Economic Analysis Vol. 11, No. 1 (June 2026) featuring articles on African countries coverage, recursive-dynamic CGE models, and MPSGE in Julia; 2026 Research Fellows (Frank van Tongeren, Niven Winchester recognized for contributions to CGE modeling, climate policy, and international trade); 2025-2028 Research Fellows including Janine Dixon, Ken Itakura, Yaghoob Jafari, and Maria Latorre recognized for contributions to CGE modeling, labor forecasting, trade policy analysis, and policy applications including Brexit and EU trade agreements.

Course offerings: GTAPinR Course (self-paced online using R packages HARr and tabloToR); 2026 GTAP Modeling Trade Policy (MTP) Course (October 12 - November 29, 2026) covering trade policy instruments using GTAP v7 and RunGTAP software; 2027 Short Course in Dynamic Global Trade Analysis (January 18 - March 12, 2027) at Purdue University for applied general equilibrium analysis using dynamic multi-region models.

Additional events: CoPS CGE Course Japan, Ruhr Grad Summer School, SIMPLE-G Short Course, GTAP MTP Course, GTAP Dynamic Short Course. Support resources include GTAP FAQs, support email (gtapsupport@purdue.edu), GTAP-L mailing list, and contact (contactgtap@purdue.edu). Featured researcher: Alexandra Hill (UC Berkeley) researching US agricultural workforce well-being and economic viability. Featured event: Modelling Extremes seminar addressing climate change and agricultural impacts across global value chains.
