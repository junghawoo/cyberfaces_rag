# Livestock and Processed Foods

Springer book chapter by Thomas W. Hertel and Uris Lantz C. Baldos (Department of Agricultural Economics, Purdue University) from "Global Change and the Challenges of Sustainably Feeding a Growing Planet" (Springer, Cham, 2016; https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-22662-0_9; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-22662-0_9; ISBN: 978-3-319-22661-3 print, 978-3-319-22662-0 online).

Chapter addresses global livestock production and demand trends. Main topics: majority of crop output consumed indirectly through processed foods or livestock products (feedstuffs); livestock demand growth in developing world tied to income gains and dietary upgrading of low-income households toward animal protein; rapid growth in pork, poultry, and dairy consumption in populous countries; shift toward intensive livestock production techniques increasing demand for feed concentrates (corn, soybeans); environmental impacts of beef (greatest footprint on land, water, greenhouse gas emissions) versus dairy, pork, and poultry (lesser impacts); understanding behavior of value-added product demand for long-run crop production projections.

Keywords: global livestock production, dietary upgrading and livestock consumption, environmental impacts of livestock production, indirect land use impacts of livestock, productivity growth in livestock. Supplemental learning resources available at https://mygeohub.org/courses/global_change.

Related content: "Livestock and Sustainable Food Systems: Status, Trends, and Priority Actions" (2023); "Environmental and Ecological Impacts of Livestock Farming" (2026); "Meat and the Environment: How Livestock Are Taking a Big Bite Out of Our Planet" (2023). Subjects covered: Agricultural Economics, Agriculture, Animal Agriculture, Crop waste, Food Production, Food Security.

References include studies on global carbon policy livestock impacts (Avetisyan et al., 2011), GHG emissions of imported versus domestic food (Avetisyan et al., 2014), land/water/GHG burdens of meat/eggs/dairy in US (Eshel et al., 2014), soybean cultivation in Brazil (Fearnside, 2001), global food market factors (Gehlhar & Regmi, 2005), climate policy livestock impacts (Golub et al., 2013), OECD R&D productivity (Griffith et al., 2004), food supply chain quality demands (King & Venturini, 2005), productivity growth in crop/livestock production (Ludena et al., 2007), and livestock industry transitions (Taheripour et al., 2013).
