# Other examples based on GEMPACK supported analysis

Foundation-level module presenting other examples of GEMPACK-supported analysis via three fetched external URL resources: GLASS, GTap, and JGEA.

**GLASS (link)** — https://mygeohub.org/groups/glass — the GLASSNET group page on MyGeohub ("Meeting the Global Sustainable Development Goals on a Changing Planet with Limited Land and Water Resources"). GLASSNET is a network providing decision makers with data to assess actions affecting the environment, economy, and local communities toward the SDGs. Page features: Featured Researcher Alexandra Hill (Assistant Professor of Cooperative Extension, University of California, Berkeley) researching the US agricultural workforce, farmworker well-being, income/health impacts, productivity, and agri-food system resilience; Featured Event: GLASSNET Thematic Seminar Series, "Modelling Extremes: From Climate Change to Biophysical Impacts and Economic Implications," on extreme weather events, agriculture/food security impacts, coinciding multi-region events, global value chains, and the global-to-local-to-global research agenda.

**GTap (link)** — https://www.gtap.agecon.purdue.edu/ — homepage of the Global Trade Analysis Project (GTAP), a global network for quantitative analysis of international policy issues, coordinated by the Center for Global Trade Analysis in Purdue University's Department of Agricultural Economics (403 Mitch Daniels Blvd., West Lafayette, IN). Content includes: GTAP Africa 1 and Africa 2 Data Bases (free; 39 regions/57 sectors of GTAP 6, and 42 regions/57 sectors of GTAP 8.1, with GTAPAgg aggregation software); GTAP 12 Data Base release; Journal of Global Economic Analysis (JGEA) Vol 11 No 1 (June 2026) with articles by Simon Mevel, Angel Aguiar, Beini Liu, Stephen Karingi (African country coverage), Wolfgang Britz and Hugo Storm (surrogate modelling of recursive-dynamic single-country CGE), and Eli Lazarus, Mitch Phillipson, David Anthoff, Thomas Rutherford (MPSGE in Julia); Research Fellow Hall of Fame inductees Frank van Tongeren (OECD METRO trade model, GTAP Scientific Council Vice Chair, Alan A. Powell Award 1999) and Niven Winchester (MIT Joint Program, Motu Economic and Public Policy Research, JGEA co-editor with Tom Hertel, Open Journal System); 2025 Alan A. Powell Award to the JRC (EU input-output tables; Pan-African Network for Economic Analysis of Policies); Research Fellows Janine Dixon (CoPS, CGE microsimulation), Ken Itakura (dynamic GTAP, non-tariff measures, CPTPP/RCEP/ASEAN), Yaghoob Jafari, and Maria Latorre (FDI, Brexit, Inflation Reduction Act). Courses: GTAPinR ("Running the GTAP Model using R packages HARr and tabloToR"), 2026 GTAP Modeling Trade Policy (MTP) Course using GTAP CGE model v7 and RunGTAP software, 2027 Short Course in Dynamic Global Trade Analysis at Purdue, and SIMPLE-G Short Course registration. Support channels: GTAP FAQs, gtapsupport@purdue.edu, contactgtap@purdue.edu, and the GTAP-L mailing list; also CoPS CGE Course Japan and Ruhr Grad Summer School notices.

## Summarized attachments
- **GLASSNET** (mygeohub.org/groups/glass, link): GLASSNET group page on MyGeohub addressing Global Sustainable Development Goals with Limited Land and Water Resources. Provides decision makers with data on environmental, economic, and community impacts. Features Alexandra Hill (UC Berkeley) researching US agricultural workforce, farmworker well-being, and agri-food system resilience. Includes GLASSNET Thematic Seminar Series on "Modelling Extremes: From Climate Change to Biophysical Impacts and Economic Implications," covering extreme weather events and their economic implications for agriculture and food security across global value chains.
- **Global Trade Analysis Project (GTAP)** (www.gtap.agecon.purdue.edu/, link): Global network at Purdue University's Center for Global Trade Analysis conducting quantitative analysis of international policy issues. Provides GTAP Africa 1 and 2 Data Bases (39-42 regions, 57 sectors), GTAP 12 Data Base release, and publishes Journal of Global Economic Analysis Vol 11 No 1 (June 2026). Lists Research Fellows including Frank van Tongeren (OECD METRO trade model), Niven Winchester (JGEA co-editor), Ken Itakura (non-tariff measures, CPTPP/RCEP/ASEAN), and Maria Latorre (Brexit, EU trade agreements). Offers courses: GTAPinR (R packages HARr and tabloToR), 2026 GTAP Modeling Trade Policy Course (GTAP v7 and RunGTAP), and 2027 Short Course in Dynamic Global Trade Analysis.
