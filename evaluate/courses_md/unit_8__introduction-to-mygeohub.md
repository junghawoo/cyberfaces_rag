In summary, MyGeoHub is ….
•
A portal to science apps and resources, hence a “Science Gateway”
•
Open access
– Anyone in the world can do the same kind of analyses this team will 
demonstrate
•
Low barrier of entry 
– Have a web browser
– No software download or installation
– No programming or GIS expertise required
•
Open source, built to support interdisciplinary research and FAIR science 
– Data and tools are Finable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable
– Increased transparency of models and analyses
– Increased reproducibility of research findings
– Create a vibrant community that share models, tools and datasets, and best 
practices.
