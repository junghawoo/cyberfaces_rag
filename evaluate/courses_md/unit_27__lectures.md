### Slide 18
Global land use impacts of technological progress in region A
Improvement in agricultural technology in A, relative to baseline, represents an outward shift in the global supply of crops, relative to no adaptation, so world price will be lower, relative to baseline
Faced by a lower world price, but unchanged technology, producers in the non-adapting rest of the world (RoW) contract production and cropland

### Slide 19

Faced by a lower world price, but unchanged technology, producers in the non-adapting rest of the world (RoW) contract production and cropland

This can arise, even if global demand elasticity for food is zero

Drought definition
Drought is lower than normal rainfall over an extended time period, usually several months or years. 
It is a natural, temporary departure from normal climate conditions, so it is about short-term variability.

Most irrigation occurs in Asia, very low in Africa – where less than 6% of cropped areas are irrigated

### Slide 28
Key findings
Irrigated croplands typically have much higher yields than their rainfed counterparts in the same Agro- Ecological Zone (AEZ); therefore, if the new lands are irrigated, less land conversion will be required
If expansion of irrigation is constrained anywhere in the world, it is likely that more cropland area will be required to meet added demands from biofuels
Furthermore, the carbon emissions factors associated with rainfed areas tend to be higher, so pushing area expansion towards these areas raises GHG emissions from biofuels expansion
Taheripour, F., Hertel, T. W., and J. Liu (2013). “The Role of Irrigation in Determining the 
Global Land Use Impacts of Biofuels”, Energy, Sustainability and Society 3:4 
doi:10.1186/2192-0567-3-4.

### Slide 30
Conclusions
Understanding the potential for expansion of irrigated areas is critical for estimation of indirect land use impacts of biofuels
If irrigated area could be expanded in all AEZs, worldwide, induced land use change from biofuels would be sharply reduced
When expansion of irrigated area is constrained, then analyses which abstract from the irrigation-rainfed distinction will underestimate the ethanol-induced land use emissions
