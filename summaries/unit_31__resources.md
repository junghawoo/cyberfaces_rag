# Resources

Foundation-level compilation of peer-reviewed scholarly resources on climate change impacts in agriculture, food security, and sustainable development, accessed via Springer Nature and MyGeoHub platforms.

**Climate Change Impacts in Agriculture (Hertel & Baldos, Springer):**
Chapter from "Global Change and the Challenges of Sustainably Feeding a Growing Planet" examines climate change mechanisms affecting crop production: temperature effects on crop development, elevated CO2 impacts on photosynthesis and respiration, water stress from temperature increases and irregular rainfall, extreme temperature damage, pest/disease pressure. Uses SIMPLE model for global simulations analyzing climate impacts on land use and food prices, explores adaptation strategies (irrigation investment, heat/drought-tolerant crop variety development). Key scientific background: IPCC reports, agricultural modeling frameworks (DSSAT cropping system model, Jones et al. 2003), and empirical evidence linking climate variability to production shocks (Lobell et al. 2013-2014, Asseng et al. 2011). References foundational works on temperature thresholds (Luo 2011), CO2 fertilization effects (Long 1991), wheat heat tolerance (Porter & Gawith 1999, Ortiz et al. 2008), and crop model intercomparison (Rosenzweig et al. 2013, AgMIP protocols). Covers maize yield sensitivity to drought and extreme heat in US Midwest, cassava production responses, and crop-specific vulnerabilities.

**Food Security and Nutrition (Hertel & Baldos, Springer):**
Addresses nine billion population feeding by 2050. Examines food security definition and measurement (undernutrition incidence/gap measures linked to poverty indices), distribution of food consumption across regions, household nutrition shortfalls, influences of agricultural productivity/biofuels/climate on nutritional outcomes using SIMPLE model. Integrates developmental economics (Foster-Greer-Thorbecke decomposable poverty measures, growth elasticities), health metrics (stunting reversibility, maternal/child undernutrition consequences, metabolic disorders), and food system vulnerability (cholera epidemiology, flood impacts in South Asia, mycotoxin contamination). References climate-food security linkages (Ahmed et al. 2009, Lobell et al. 2008), agricultural productivity research (Alston et al. 2009, Fuglie 2012), and multi-country studies on food access and income elasticity patterns.

**GLASSNET Resources (MyGeoHub):**
Global Land-use And Food Security on a changing planet research network addressing UN Sustainable Development Goals. Featured researcher Alexandra Hill (UC Berkeley) studies US agricultural workforce well-being, farmworker impacts on business performance (productivity, profitability, workplace safety), and agri-food system resilience. Thematic seminar series "Modelling Extremes" examines extreme weather event propagation through global value chains, improved climate-to-economics modeling chains, adaptation options evaluation across scales.

**Supplemental Materials:** Free learning resources available at mygeohub.org/courses/global_change

**Themes:** Climate sensitivity (1.5-4.5°C per doubling CO2), climate models (CESM, GFDL, HadCM3, global circulation models), RCP scenarios (2.6, 4.5, 6.0, 8.5), agricultural risk assessment, food system resilience, research-development policy for climate-smart agriculture.

## Summarized attachments

- **Climate Change Impacts in Agriculture** (https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-22662-0_6, Springer chapter): Peer-reviewed book chapter by Hertel and Baldos examining climate change mechanisms affecting crop production including temperature effects on development, CO2 impacts on photosynthesis and respiration, water stress, extreme temperature damage, and pest pressure, using SIMPLE model for global simulations and exploring adaptation strategies.
- **Food Security and Nutrition** (https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-22662-0_10, Springer chapter): Scholarly chapter addressing global food security and nutritional outcomes for nine billion people by 2050, examining definition/measurement of food security, distribution of consumption, household shortfalls, and influences of agricultural productivity, biofuels, and climate change on nutrition.
- **GLASS Resources** (https://mygeohub.org/groups/glass, research network): MyGeoHub-hosted Global Land-use And Food Security research network addressing UN Sustainable Development Goals with focus on climate extremes modeling, value chain impacts, and adaptation evaluation across scales.
