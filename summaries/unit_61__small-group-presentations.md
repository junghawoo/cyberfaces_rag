# Small group presentations (will be uploaded after the short course).

This unit collects three small-group presentation PDFs (hosted on Dropbox) from the 2022 SIMPLE-G Short Course in Multi-scale Analysis of Sustainability (May 2-6, 2022), each applying the SIMPLE / SIMPLE-G global agricultural economics model.

Group 1 Presentation (`Group_1_PPT_2022 SIMPLE-G short course.pdf`) — four talks:
1. Taís Menezes, "The Threat of Plant Disease Outbreaks to Global Food Security: An Application of the SIMPLE Model" (May 6, 2022). Cites yield-loss estimates from FAO 2019 (20-40%), Wang et al. 2021 (13-22%), Rizzo et al. 2021 (30%), Miranda et al. 2016 (14.3-34.3% for soybeans, corn, cotton in Brazil), plus Ristaino et al. 2021 (PNAS) and Savary et al. 2019 (Nature Ecology and Evolution). Uses SIMPLE equations E_AOCROPg, E_QLANDg, E_QNLAND, E_QCROPg (zero profit condition) with variables p_AOCROP, ECROP, SHRLANDg/SHRNLANDg. Simulates a 2017 Brazil outbreak (shocks -14.33% and -34.30%) reporting p_QCROP, p_PCROP, p_YIELD, p_QLAND, p_PLAND, p_QNLAND, p_PNLAND, p_MAL_COUNT for World/BRA/E_Euro/N_Afr, and a 2050 Latin America outbreak (BRA, S_Amer, CC_Amer) with subtotals by pop, inc, tfp_crop, disease across 17 regions — 5,509,640 more undernourished people. Future project: Brazilian pests/diseases by crop (soybean bedbug, Helicoverpa armigera, whitefly, false-medideira caterpillar, rust; maize fall armyworms; cotton boll weevil, leafworm caterpillar, cotton aphid) and phytosanitary barrier effects.
2. Shellye Suttles, "Farm-to-Fork Strategy / European Green Deal" (6 May 2022). Six scenarios: BAU, CHEM (20% chemical input cut, slack_pnland("EU")=-20), ANTI (50% antimicrobial sales cut, p_QNCRPFEED("EU")=-5), ORGC (25% organic cropland, p_AOCROPr_cc("EU")=-4), DIET (red meat reduction, slack_q_pc("Livestock","EU")=-25), ALLS (all combined); maps each to SIMPLE equations (E_QNLANDg with ENLANDg, crop-feed equations with ECRPFEED, QLAND/QNLAND/PCROP, p_QPC with EOP and EINC). Results on EU prices, undernutrition count, cropland use margins, and EU GHG emission sources; prior analyses cited: Fuglie et al. 2022, Beckman et al. 2020, Cortigani et al. 2022.
3. Diego S. Cardoso, "Climate-efficient Agriculture R&D." Builds on the AEPP paper (Fuglie et al., 2022): scenarios S0 (BAU) and S1-S4 (R&D growth 1-4% annually); computes average and marginal GHG abatement costs under accounting-cost and total-welfare metrics; concludes producer (land-owner) losses exceed consumer gains as land values fall sharply.
4. Hermen Luchtenbelt, "SIMPLE-G: Heartland corn shock." CMIP6 maize/soybean productivity declines vs wheat gains (Jägermeyr, 2021); 2017 economy, Heartland yield shock over 11,359 grids, AOCROP -15%; gridded differences in P_QLANDj, ELANDj, P_PLANDj, QCROPj, expansion vs substitution effects, and regional spillovers via SHRQSCROPr and ELANDr.
5. Cicero Lima, "MC Simulation and SIMPLE" (Group #1, May 2022). Monte Carlo simulation over uncertain land supply elasticity using SIMPLE-G-BRAZIL; TFP shocks from the AEPP paper for S1/S2/S3 scenarios across 17 regions; regional results for Brazil and China after n=100 runs, global crop output/price variance (SE_Asia highest), and global GHG emissions sensitivity.

Group 2 Presentation (`Group_2_PPT_2022 SIMPLE-G short course.pdf`): Arlie Fachinello, Cristina Vaquero Pineiro, Jonathan Doelman, Louise Roos (guidance by Iman Haqiqi), "Restricting ground water use for irrigation purposes in the USA." Four simulations: Sim1 baseline world in 2050 (population, income, livestock/processed-food productivity, crop productivity without R&D), Sim2 climate change, Sim3 groundwater restrictions (negative shock to 2,713 grid cells, avg -3.3%, min -0.22% to max -90%, via s_QWATGRDgl(GRID,"Irrigated")), Sim4 R&D response. Groundwater-surface water substitution (elasticity 0.5); results for p_QLANDr, p_QCROPr, p_PCROPr, p_QWATSGr across 16 regions, consumption and consumer prices for Crops/Livestock/Proc_Food/Non_Food/Biofuel, US subregions (Heartland, NorthCrescent, NorthGreatPlains, PrairieGateway, EastUplands, SouthSeaboard, FruitfulRim, BasinRange, MississippiPortal), and grid-level anomalies (cell I02321 QLANDgl/VLANDgl irrigated vs rainfed). Key methodological point: combining all shocks at once with subtotals vs applying shocks step-by-step gives different results — "It matters!"

Group 3 Presentation (`Group_3_PPT_2022 SIMPLE-G short course.pdf`): Maksym Chepeliev, Kelsie Ferin, Ziqian Gong, Angelo Gurgel and Michael Jeri (guidance: Jing Liu and Zhan Wang), "Impacts of Nitrogen Policies and Climate Change on the US Market." Uses SIMPLE-G-US-CS (corn-soy) model with 48,317 US grid cells and Gompertz curves per grid cell supplied by the Agro-IBIS model linking nitrogen application, leaching and yields. Sample experiment: 100% nitrogen leaching tax → 9% leaching reduction, 2.1% crop output reduction, land use -0.9% (rainfed -0.96%, irrigated -0.43%). N fertilizer input tax experiment: exogenize QNLEACHr("US") target -9.02%, endogenize t_PNITROr("US") via swap (equation E_PNITROr with ENITROr) → fertilizer tax +27.4%; compares NLTax vs FertTax at national, state (25 states = 98% of corn-soy output), and grid level (cells I01774 California low leaching; I48438 Missouri high leaching). Climate change scenario: TFP shocks of -10.6% (rainfed) and -1% (irrigated) → irrigated land +7.85%, higher N leaching and prices; combined climate + optimized fertilizer tax needs ~12% leaching reduction (N leach 3,433,109.5 MT vs 3,859,019.3 MT) and a ~39.8% fertilizer tax.

## Summarized attachments

- **Group 1 Presentation** (https://www.dropbox.com/s/4kgtdc5nwptmd03/Group_1_PPT_2022%20SIMPLE-G%20short%20course.pdf?dl=0, PDF): Five related presentations from the 2022 SIMPLE-G Short Course on applying the SIMPLE agricultural economic model to diverse sustainability questions including plant disease impacts on food security, European Green Deal farm-to-fork strategy, climate-efficient agricultural R&D, regional climate shocks, and Monte Carlo simulation of uncertain parameters.

- **Group 2 Presentation** (https://www.dropbox.com/s/cgkl08tsl22jfpf/Group_2_PPT_2022%20SIMPLE-G%20short%20course.pdf?dl=0, PDF): Presentation on restricting groundwater use for irrigation in the USA, modeling scenarios including baseline, climate change, groundwater restrictions, and R&D responses using SIMPLE-G with gridded water availability constraints and evaluating regional impacts on land use, crop production, and consumer welfare across US agricultural regions.

- **Group 3 Presentation** (https://www.dropbox.com/s/qoov7vqzvfnzh2v/Group_3_PPT_2022%20SIMPLE-G%20short%20course.pdf?dl=0, PDF): Presentation on nitrogen policies and climate change impacts on US corn and soybean markets using gridded SIMPLE-G model with field-level nitrogen application, leaching, and yield relationships, comparing nitrogen leaching taxes versus fertilizer input taxes under current and climate-stressed conditions.
